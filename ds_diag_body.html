Irish Lotto: checking details that are easy to miss

The draw date selects the historical format

Irish Lotto has changed both its number range and draw calendar. The analysis uses the stored effective date of those changes. A historical result with a number that is not in today's range is not rejected merely for being historical.

Lotto Plus results need their own checks

The main Lotto set and Bonus Ball do not establish Lotto Plus outcomes. Use the specific draw name and date for every additional game rather than copying a match from one result into another.

Compare dates before comparing a headline amount

A next-draw advertisement and the jackpot associated with a collected result refer to different events. Preserve the euro label, payment context and draw date when recording an amount. An absent amount is not interpreted as zero or as a rollover.

Keep the financial decision separate

A result is not a bank balance. Before committing money, establish whether a claim is valid, what the payment represents and which currency applies. Do not borrow against an assumed win or use a possible future prize as recurring income.
